Why identity photos deserve local processing

A passport photo is a biometric template: a straight-on view of your face, usually with your name attached to the file. Uploading it to a free background remover means trusting a stranger's retention policy, and "we delete uploads after one hour" is not something you can verify.

CutLocal flips the model around. The AI weights come to you, the pixels stay where they are, and there is no server that could keep a copy even if it wanted to. That is also why it keeps working offline, on a plane, or on a locked-down work laptop.

Practical notes for a photo that gets accepted:

  • Face centred, eyes open, neutral expression, no glasses glare.
  • No shadow behind the head — the background remover can't invent an even backdrop if the subject was lit from one side.
  • Keep the natural head size; cropping too tight is a common rejection reason.
  • Export as high-quality JPEG, then check the printed size your authority asks for.

CutLocal is not a visa service and does not guarantee acceptance — always check your authority's current rules.

Questions people ask

Can I change the background of a passport photo online safely?

Only if the photo never leaves your device. Passport photos are identity documents, so uploading them to an unknown service is a real privacy risk. CutLocal processes the image in your browser: the file is read from disk, segmented locally and written back to disk. Nothing is sent to a server, which you can confirm in your browser's Network tab.

What size should a passport photo be?

Requirements vary: the US asks for 2 × 2 inches (600 × 600 px at 300 dpi), the UK and most of the EU ask for 35 × 45 mm (413 × 531 px at 300 dpi), India asks for 2 × 2 inches. This page presets 600 × 600 with a white background; switch the size preset to Custom to enter 413 × 531 or your country's exact figure.

Which background colour is accepted?

White or off-white for most countries, including the US, UK, Canada, Australia and India. A few countries accept light blue or light grey. Pick white first, and only change it if your specific authority asks for another colour.

Will the cutout keep my hair edges intact?

The IS-Net model produces soft alpha edges, so flyaway hair is mostly preserved. For official photos, check the result at 100% zoom: authorities reject visible halos or a cut-out look. A clean, evenly lit photo against a plain wall gives the best result.
