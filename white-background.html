What marketplaces actually check

Marketplace moderation is mostly automated, and the rules for the main product image are consistent across platforms:

  • Pure white background — 255, 255, 255, not off-white, not a photo of a studio wall.
  • At least 1000 px on the longest side so the zoom feature works. 1080 or 1600 px is a safe target.
  • The product fills the frame — Amazon asks for roughly 85%. That is why the White preset adds only a few percent of padding.
  • The whole product visible — no cropped edges, no props, no text overlays.

A photo taken on a phone against a coloured wall usually passes once the background is replaced, as long as the lighting is even. If the original is backlit or badly shadowed, the cutout is still clean but the product itself keeps its original lighting.

Questions people ask

What white does a marketplace require for product photos?

Amazon requires a pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255) for its main image, with the product filling at least 85% of the frame and 1000 px or more on the longest side. Etsy, eBay and Shopify are more relaxed but bright, even backgrounds sell better. CutLocal's White preset sets exactly #FFFFFF and a 1080 × 1080 canvas with a small padding so the product is centred.

Can I process a whole catalogue at once?

Yes. Drop or select as many photos as you like, pick the White preset once, then download everything as a single ZIP. All processing happens on your machine, so there is no queue and no per-image limit.

Will the white background be pure white?

Yes. The subject is cut out and composited onto #FFFFFF, so the result is exactly 255, 255, 255. Export as JPEG at high quality for marketplaces, or PNG if you need lossless edges.

Are my product photos uploaded to a server?

No. The background removal model runs inside your browser. That matters for unreleased products, client work under NDA, or anything you would rather not send to a third-party service.
