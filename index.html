How a local background remover works

Most background removers send your photo to a server, process it there and send back the result. CutLocal downloads the model to you instead, then does the work on your own hardware — the same way a photo editor does.

01 / LOAD

The model comes to you

By the time the page has painted, your browser is already fetching the IS-Net model (~88 MB on the default Balanced setting, 44 MB on Fast) and caching it. That is the only download that ever happens.

02 / RUN

Your CPU does the inference

The model runs in WebAssembly on your machine, building an alpha mask pixel by pixel. Your image data never leaves the tab.

03 / EXPORT

Full resolution, your format

Add a colour, a photo background, padding, a square crop or a resize. Download one file, copy it to the clipboard, or export the whole batch as ZIP.

Questions people ask

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. The AI model runs inside your browser using WebAssembly, so the image you drop is read from local memory and never sent over the network. You can verify it yourself: open your browser's Network tab, process an image, and watch that zero image requests are made. The only downloads are the AI model files, once.

Is CutLocal really free and unlimited?

Yes. There is no server doing the work, so there is no per-image cost, no sign-up, no watermark and no daily limit. The whole tool is a static page plus a model file your browser caches.

Why does it download about 100 MB the first time?

The neural network that separates the subject from the background has to be on your device to run locally. The default Balanced model is about 88 MB and the browser engine files about 12 MB; the lighter Fast model is 44 MB. It starts downloading as soon as the page opens, so it is usually ready by the time you pick a photo, and it is cached for every later visit — including offline.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the engine has been cached. Install CutLocal as an app from your browser's address bar and it keeps working with no connection at all.

How good is the cutout quality compared with remove.bg or PhotoRoom?

CutLocal uses the IS-Net segmentation model, the same family of models used by many commercial background removers. On people, products, cars and animals it produces clean edges including hair detail. Very complex edges (fine fur against a busy background, transparent glass) still need manual touch-up, as with any automatic tool.

What image formats and sizes are supported?

JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF and BMP up to your browser's canvas limits (usually well above 4000 px). You can export PNG with transparency, WebP for smaller files, or JPEG on a solid background, and resize to a square, a fixed longest side or a custom size.

Can I use the results commercially?

Yes. You keep all rights to your images and the output is yours to use anywhere, including client work and product listings. No attribution is required.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in Chrome on Android and Safari on iOS 17+. Large models are a big download on mobile data, so the Fast model is the default there.
